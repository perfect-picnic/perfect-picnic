About us

Picnics, made easy

Hi, I'm Regina. I've called Amsterdam home for five years, and by day I work in accounting — which means a lot of hours behind a screen. Picnics became my way of switching that off: no fixed plans, no itinerary, just wandering wherever the mood takes me with good snacks in hand. I fell in love with how naturally the Dutch build picnics into everyday life — once, I even turned my own birthday into a picnic with my family on the Champ de Mars in Paris. What I love most is watching people put their phones down and just enjoy being together. That's the whole idea behind Perfect Picnic.

FAQ

Good to know

Where do we meet?

Our fixed meeting point is the south entrance to Vondelpark at Van Eeghenstraat 80, 1071 GK Amsterdam — this leads straight into the park centre, where the best picnic spots are. We'll hand you your packed set right there — blanket included where applicable — so you can walk in and choose your own spot.

Do you only operate in Vondelpark?

For now, yes — we hand off picnic sets at Vondelpark's south entrance. Message us if you're hoping for another spot in the city and we'll see if we can make it work.

How does payment work?

Pay securely online when you book. Once your payment goes through, send us your date, time and details through the form below and we'll confirm.

Can you handle allergies or dietary needs?

Yes — mention it in your booking notes and we'll adjust the box where we can.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before your picnic. Inside 24 hours, we're not able to offer a refund — but message us and we'll always try to help if something comes up.

What if the weather turns bad?

If it's rained out, it's your choice — we'll either reschedule you to another day or refund your booking in full. Just message us as soon as you can.
